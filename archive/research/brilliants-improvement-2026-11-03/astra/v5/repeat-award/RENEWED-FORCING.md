# Bounded renewed-forcing inspection

The precise checking-fork proof rescues both duplicated true Nb5 records without rescuing any of the eight false continuations in this 11-case selected set. It does NOT rescue true Qh6 on the premise of a newly created mating threat: the same Qg7# after ...gxh5 already existed at the prior awarded Rxh5+. Thus the proposed common explanation is only partly supported.

All positions come from saved legal PGN boards. No engine searches, source-classifier variants, or app changes were made. Earlier origins are the exact confirmed frozen-v4 predictions from the completed repeat audit. These selected development examples cannot establish a new generalization result.

## Concrete definition and bounded checks

For each same offered victim, enumerate each legal acceptance that clears that decision's capture/promotion credit plus100 under native-compatible raw SEE. After acceptance enumerate legal checks. Record literal checkmate, or a checking fork whose checking piece can make a legal next capture after EVERY legal check evasion. Count physical material gained by check and next capture, subtract material captured by the evasion and same-square SEE recapture liability after the final capture; require net gain greater than100. A next capture by the checking piece must actually exist for every evasion; a checking capture that merely wins material immediately is not mislabeled a fork. This distinguishes forced recovery from a gross material count that ignores recapture.

The 512-node bound is shared across acceptance SEE and check/evasion/final-SEE branches for each examined decision/victim. Every one completed, maximum127 counted nodes. This is bounded board proof, not an engine claim of a forced win; cross-square later combinations remain outside its scope. forcing-safe-fork.py/json contain all branches. forcing.py/json preserve the initial gross-capture diagnostic to make its limitation explicit.

## Important counterexamples and ambiguity

False Bc5 superficially supplies ...Bxf6 Rxe6+ followed by Rxf6/Rxe7 after every evasion. Gross capture counting claims390 recovery. However ...Kg7 or ...Kf7 allows the capturing rook to be recaptured, leaving negative110 net on the same-square exchange. Including legal recapture refutes the apparent new fork. False Rh1 has newly available checking captures, but they do not establish a subsequent capture by that checking piece against every response. A generic forced-material check or arbitrary next-capture test is broader than the tested fork proof and should not inherit its result.

Qh6 is the decisive remaining true counterexample. The prior Qg5 already answers ...gxh5 with Qg7#. Qh6 moves that queen out of the fifth-rank path, newly allowing Black's Qc5 to take Rh5; ...Qxh5 also loses to Qg7#. Calling this a renewed *acceptance-specific* mating proof would exempt Qh6 here, but it is not a newly created mating threat. Comparing raw UCI strings (g5g7 versus h6g7) would spuriously label the same mating destination as novel. A mechanism that treats newly exposed acceptance routes as novelty must say so explicitly and be evaluated beyond these selected counterexamples; this audit does not authorize or establish that classifier.

Recommendation: the safe new checking-fork witness is a coherent limited exception with concrete evidence for Nb5. Keep Qh6 fully disclosed as a counterexample to strict threat novelty. The selected eleven do not warrant claiming the entire repeat rule can be made recall-free. No further searches or rule variants were run.
