## Exact rule and evidence

The source remains baseline A for all ordinary classifications, quality, phase, material and full-window confirmation gates. The sequence restriction can only remove a confirmed A Brilliant. Current checking moves, captures, castling and promotions are preserved. A current quiet award is suppressed when one of the previous three own decisions was a confirmed BASE A Brilliant and covers every currently qualifying offered physical piece. Each piece must remain unmoved/unreplaced throughout the actual intervening PGN, have qualified against the earlier move's capture/promotion credit, and retain the same raw exchange loss after every intervening own move.

That origin cannot justify suppression if either specified exception is proved for the same offered victim:

1. A currently profitable legal acceptance was not legal after that earlier awarded decision and now has a literal legal mate-in-one reply. An already existing mate after an already existing acceptance is insufficient. The acceptance itself is tested against current credit+100.
2. A new checking-fork reply forces the checking piece to make a subsequent capture after every legal check evasion. Check gain plus the best such capture, less the evasion's capture and final same-square exchange liability, must exceed100. Fork novelty compares checking-piece role and destination against proofs after profitable acceptances of that same earlier victim. This recognizes a new checking destination without treating a mere changed from-square as a new threat.

Fork novelty is deliberately coarse: role plus checking destination, not exact threat identity.
