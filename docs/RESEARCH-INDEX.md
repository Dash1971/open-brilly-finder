## Research appendix

The previous classifier, Mute Selective, is documented in its [own study](../studies/brilliant-selective-2026-11-06/REPORT.md), with the gallery collection, the 1,000-game native test and the complete scientific archive. Mute Exchange reuses its games, labels and test sample.

The [archived research index](HISTORICAL-RESEARCH-INDEX.md) lists the individual investigations, alternative methods and earlier models. Their original results are preserved for reproducibility. The report and algorithm linked above describe the selected model.
