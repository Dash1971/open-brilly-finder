SAVED-LINE TACTICAL AUDIT OF THE FIXED 12-PAIR PANEL

Scope: PANEL-EVIDENCE.json, its FENs, inventories, current/previous saved
continuations, and listed acceptance branches only. No engine run, rule change,
refit, or later game continuation used as a predictor. The source reports 208
legally replayed lines. Scores quoted below are WHITE-oriented centipawns;
positive is good for White even when Black made the candidate move. Forest scores
are descriptive held-out scores, not chess evaluations or award probabilities.

The two EC-false near pairs (1-2) have no exact-anchor ordinary candidates and
have distances about .269. They are poor tactical controls. Pairs 3-12 match the
prespecified semantic anchors, not full positions or feature vectors. They can
expose information lost in summaries; they cannot establish why a label differs.

PAIR | MOVES AND FOREST SCORES (Brilliant miss / explicit ordinary) | SAVED TACTICAL EVIDENCE AND LIMIT

1 | Black Qf1+ .818 / Black Ng4+ .780; distance .269, NOT exact.
  Qf1+ places the moved queen on f1: Rxf1 Rxf1# is a forced short mating
  mechanism, with the f8 rook delivering mate. Ng4+ instead moves the e5 knight
  off the c7-g3 diagonal, uncovering a queen check against Kg3 while offering
  the unmoved Qc7: Nxc7 Bf2#. Its best saved defensive line also mates
  (Kh4 Qd8+ Ne7+ Qxe7+ Kh5 Qg5#). Both have mating tactics, but moved/offered
  piece, accepting move, mate distance, material treatment and alternatives
  differ. This pair cannot diagnose the low score as a label contradiction.

2 | White Rxg7+ .828 / Black Nxd5 .515; distance .269, NOT exact.
  Rxg7+ Bxg7 Qxg7# is another immediate mating acceptance. The ordinary
  Nxd5 offers a knight to either Nxd5 or exd5; ...Qxc5 takes White's bishop
  (with check after Nxd5), and the exd5 branch continues ...Qxc5 gxf6 Qxb4.
  The latter is a material/queen counterattack with finite evaluation, not
  a matched short-mate sacrifice. No exact near EC-false ordinary existed.

3 | White Bxg6 .798 / White Bxd5 .568; exact anchors, distance .064.
  Bxg6 attacks the castled king's pawn cover. Accepting with ...fxg6 opens
  the f-file and permits Qxg6+ Rg7 Qxe6+ Qf7 Rxf7 Rxf7 g6, saved at +703.
  White can sustain an attack/passed g-pawn; declining with ...Qb7 instead
  yields the slower Be4/Qh7+/g6/g7 plan. Ordinary Bxd5 Bxd5 vacates the
  c-file so Rxc8 takes Black's queen, ...Rbxc8 Qxd4 then continues a material
  liquidation (+315). Different compensation attribution is visible, but
  both are calculable acceptance tactics. Numeric debt/gap differences are
  already encoded; the motifs alone are not a proven award boundary.

4 | White Bxg6 .796 / White Bxc5 .563; exact anchors, distance .068.
  Bxg6 hxg6 Qxg6+ Kh8 opens the h-pawn shield and gives queen access to g6;
  O-O develops the rook into the attack. Saved play includes Qh6+/exf6 and
  eventual Qxf6 trades (+402 acceptance PV). Ordinary Bxc5 Nxc5 vacates
  the d-file: Rxd8+ Rxd8 Ba2 takes the queen and continues a material trade
  (+253). Attacking exposure versus opened-file liquidation is a concrete
  difference, not evidence that only the former deserves Brilliant.

5 | Black Nxe4 .452 / Black Nxg3 .429; exact anchors, distance .037.
  Nxe4 has two materially different acceptances. Bxe4 removes Bd3 from the
  d-file: ...Qxd1 Rxd1 Rxe4 restores material. Rxe4 instead allows ...Rxe4
  and a different liquidation. Nxg3 offers two knights: hxg3 permits
  ...Qxh1, fxe5 permits ...Qxd4, and Qxe5 permits ...Nxh1+. The control's
  branch graph is at least as tactically rich. Both post-move main scores
  are near equality (+16/+44 White). The saved lines give no clean chess
  rationale for awarding only Nxe4; unresolved label distinction.

6 | Black Nxf4 .475 / White Nxf7 .539; exact anchors, distance .040.
  In the queenless Brilliant position, Bxf4 allows ...Bxe5 Bxe5+ Rxe5;
  Black recovers White's Ne5 and trades bishops. Best defense is instead
  Nxd7+ Rxd7, a countercapture with check, near equality (+7 White).
  Ordinary Nxf7 accepts ...Kxf7 or ...Rxf7; Bxd5(+ when the king captured)
  then removes Nd5, with queen checks and activity in the king-capture line.
  Both depend on an intermediate recovery rather than simply losing the
  offered knight. The large composition difference (queens absent/present)
  and resulting tempo structure are real; neither establishes the label.

7 | Black Nxf4 .813 / Black Nxe4 .642; exact anchors, distance .032.
  Nxf4 Rxf4 e5 is an immediate pawn fork of Rf4 and Bd4. After Rff1 exd4
  Nxd4 the material is restored and play is roughly level (-5 White in the
  acceptance PV). Another saved choice is ...Nxd3 Qxd3 e5, changing the
  order of recovery. Ordinary Nxe4 Nxe4 Qe7 pins Ne4 on the e-file before
  ...d5 attacks both Ne4 and Nd4; the acceptance PV is +57 White.
  Both use a pawn fork to recover a knight investment. Rook/bishop versus
  two-knight targets and immediate versus prepared fork are lost in scalar
  summaries. This is a close counterexample to any generic fork criterion.

8 | White Bxd6 .803 / Black Bxc6 .645; exact anchors, distance .063.
  Bxd6 exd6 Rf4 targets Bf6 with Rxf6+; the saved acceptance line gives
  White +72, but Black's best post-move line is Rc1+ Ke2 Rc2+ Kf1 (0),
  declining the bishop and retaining checks. Ordinary Bxc6 dxc6 Rxc6 gives
  up a bishop for the c6/d5 pawns and active rook play (+57 White, slightly
  unfavorable for the Black mover). These are very different endgames:
  2R+B versus 2R+B, compared with R+B versus N+B. Attack on a remaining
  bishop and the option to keep checking differ from pawn liquidation.
  The converted-material flag and phase already capture part, not all, of
  this difference; no new cutoff follows from it.

9 | White Bg2 .762 / White Bh6 .759; exact anchors, distance .043.
  Bg2 permits ...Bxb1 Rxb1 Rb6 Qxh7 Ng6 Rxb6 Bxb6: an exchange investment

  Ordinary Bh6 has the more forcing acceptance story: ...Bxe7 dxe7 Ra8
  e8=Q+ Rxe8 Qf7, followed by Bg5/Bf6+ and recovery of Black's queen

  The ordinary is not devoid of sacrificial compensation or forcing play.
  This reverse example blocks a simple 'promotion/mating threat means
  Brilliant' explanation; the two forest scores are nearly identical.

10 | Black Nf6 .747 / Black Ng4 .685; exact anchors, distance .070.
  Both leave an already offered bishop. Nf6 fxe4 Bc5 pins White's
  Qd4 against Kf2: Qxc5 Nxe4+ Ke1 Nxc5, winning back the queen after a knight
  check (-335 White). Ordinary Ng4 gxh3 Qh2# is immediate mate. The ordinary

  Thus acceptance mate is not sufficient to explain the award. Its current
  mating_acceptance and physical_mate_leaf flags are nevertheless false:
  their route-specific definitions must be understood before interpreting
  those booleans as 'no acceptance line mates'. This is a representation

11 | Black Ba6 .507 / White Bd3 .610; exact anchors, distance .044.
  Ba6 attacks Rd3 while leaving Rd4 available. Nxd4 exd4 creates a pawn
  fork of Qe3 and Rc3; saved Rc7 Qxc7 gives Black -436 White. Choosing
  ...Rxd4 instead is +308 White, so recovery choice matters greatly.
  Ordinary Bd3 leaves Rd4 to ...Bxd4 but recaptures Nxd4, restoring the
  bishop for an exchange, +155 White. Its best defense uses ...Ncxb2/
  ...Naxb2 counterplay instead. Both have a preexisting rook offer; one
  uses an indirect pawn fork and the other direct recapture. This difference
  is a possible attribution descriptor, not an established label criterion.

12 | White Bc4 .727 / White Qg5 .792; exact anchors, distance .055.
  Bc4 leaves Nc3 to ...dxc3, but Qxf7+ Kd7 dxc3 Kc7 Bf4+ begins a king
  hunt (+292); Bxf7+ is also a saved strong alternative (+279). Black's

  Ordinary Qg5 leaves Na4 to ...Bxa4 and obtains Nxh5+ Kh7 Nf6+ Kg7 f4,
  also a forcing attack (+118). Both offer an unmoved knight and exploit
  checks; opening f7 access versus h-file/knight invasion differs. The
  ordinary's score is higher. No simple 'quiet move plus king attack'
  account resolves their labels.

WHAT IS PRESENT IN SAVED LINES BUT COMPRESSED OUT OF THE 75 FEATURES

* Branch topology: which acceptance forces which reply, whether recovery is
  direct or delayed, whether the opponent can decline with checks, and whether
  different acceptances need different mechanisms. Counts/minima/maxima do not
  retain target identity or move order (pairs 5, 7, 8, 11).
* Compensation attribution: opening a queen's capture file, exposing a king,
  forking a queen and rook, regaining a knight, creating promotion threats, or
  exchanging into a particular material composition can share similar debt and
  evaluation summaries. The actual target squares, attacker roles and lines
  of force are recoverable here, but not represented explicitly by the scalar
  features (3-4, 6-12).
* Reply-quality distribution: a forced mating reply and an equalizing best
  defense can coexist with a spectacular acceptance punishment; pair 9's
  ordinary and pair 10's ordinary are especially clear. The source's root-gap,
  safe-alternative and mating booleans retain some related information; they
  do not reproduce the complete conditional tree. Two saved candidate lines
  are not an exhaustive legal-reply distribution either.
* Composition: phase/material totals omit which side owns the surviving
  bishops, knights and rooks and how those pieces support the mechanism.
  Pair 6 and pair 8 are visibly different tactical settings despite anchor
  matches. This is a hypothesis about representation, not proof that richer
  composition would generalize or explain the labels.

LIMITS AND NEXT INFORMATION NEEDED BEFORE A NEW RULE

This selected panel diagnoses neither all 429 misses nor a causal source of the
forest scores. Generic material and gap extensions have already failed; these
examples do not justify another such extension or a new motif OR-list. Several
ordinary controls have equally concrete, sometimes more forcing, compensation.
The saved evidence supports describing missing mechanism/branch structure, not
claiming the external award criterion has been found.

Before any new rule, distinguish the source helper semantics from literal
chess meanings (notably pair 10's mating flags), preserve which side and target
receive each intermediate gain, and account for the ordinary reverse examples
5/7/9/10/12. Also retain the control-quality failure for 1-2. The unresolved
information is why the external labels differ when both moves have credible
sacrifice mechanisms, and whether any proposed descriptor separates those labels
outside this fixed descriptive panel. Neither can be resolved by the existence
of a colorful saved line alone. Actual later gameplay is not evidence of a
pre-move prediction feature.
