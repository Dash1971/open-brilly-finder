RESULT

The tested consistency change does not meet the objective. It recovers four
Brilliants, loses two correct awards, and adds two wrong awards. Keep the
existing policy. No application change, deployment, forest refit, feature
change, or threshold adjustment was made.

The original primary policy misses465 of917 saved Chess.com Brilliants:

  429 pass every eligibility check but score below the forest cutoff.
   26 fail an eligibility check AND score below the forest cutoff.
   10 score high enough but fail an eligibility check.

The36 misses with gate failures consist of31 quality failures and5 cases with
no qualifying raw offer. None fail confidence, physical-known, resulting-score
floor, or two-root checks. All10 above-cutoff gate-blocked misses are quality
failures. The other21 quality failures also have low forest scores; all5
raw-offer failures have low scores.

Thus even an oracle that recovered all above-cutoff gate-blocked positives
would add at most10 Brilliants with these scores/cutoffs unchanged, taking
452/917 to462/917 (50.38%). That is a diagnostic upper bound on gate-only
recovery, not a proposed rule or attainable error-free result. Correcting the
evaluation features fed into the forest, or retraining it, is outside this
bound and outside this experiment.

The429 eligible score misses include245 for which the En Croissant sacrifice
feature is true and184 for which it is false. Their corresponding correct

WHAT THE TEST CHANGED

Original quality compares the best score before the move with the best score
from an independent search after the move. Different searches/depths can
disagree about the same move.

The candidate uses the best and played-move scores from the SAME before-position
search instead. It retains the existing five-percentage-point tolerance and
score mapping. This substitution applies only when the played root and its
score are present in BOTH complete saved windows. Otherwise the entire pair
retains the original quality policy. Both windows must pass.

All75 forest inputs, missing indicators, saved minimum-of-two-window scores,
cutoffs, raw-offer requirement, after-score floor, two-root requirement,
confidence check, and physical-unknown abstention remain unchanged. In
particular, the forest still sees the original loss features. This deliberately
isolates the external quality check; it does not correct every evaluation-based
measurement in the classifier.

Same-root pairs are available for836/917 positives and2,206/2,807 negatives.
The81 positive and601 negative missing pairs retain the original policy.
After all unchanged guards, eligibility changes are:
  Positives: seven admitted, six removed.
  Negatives: seven admitted, two removed.
The forest score then rejects some of these changes, yielding the award counts
above. All10 high-scoring quality-blocked positives have complete root pairs:
four become eligible; the other six still exceed the same-root loss limit.

EVALUATION AND VERIFICATION

Same3,724 cases,917 positives,2,807 negatives,972 game/position groups. Exact
prior five outer folds and three-inner-fold-selected cutoffs; aliases, repeated
canonical positions, whole games, and both windows retain their groups.
No new selection was performed, so the inner fits do not need to be rerun.
Original primary and fixed-control predictions reproduce exactly on all rows.
Protocol and script/input hashes were frozen before computing candidate awards.
There were zero new engine searches and zero model fits.

An independent read-only recomputation, without importing evaluate.py, verified
all3,724 rows' scores, gates, thresholds, awards, grouping and provenance. It
also checked the miss partitions and all eight changed primary decisions.

The conditional paired whole-group bootstrap95% interval for recall change is
approximately -0.32 to+0.77 percentage points; the wrong-count change interval
is0 to+5. These intervals condition on frozen predictions and do not include
model refitting or repeated-research uncertainty. The point result already
fails the stated criterion; the intervals do not establish equal future errors.

LIMITS AND IMPLICATION

The saved dataset is enriched and repeatedly studied. Group isolation prevents
original training/test overlap within each fit, but does not turn this reused
research dataset into an untouched prospective test or a live-site precision
estimate. The old fitting procedure used eligible training rows; admitting
previously ineligible cases changes that selection. The fixed OOF scores still
provide the intended controlled comparison, but not validation of a newly
trained system under the revised policy.

This test uses saved search strength. Same-search measurements can still be
shallow or wrong. Deeper consistent searches and replacing evaluation inputs
were not tested, and this result does not rule out their value. It does show
that the proposed gate-only repair, as frozen, is insufficient and increases
wrong awards. Evaluation-gate errors explain only a small portion of the
current missed-positive count at the existing forest scores.

The next substantial recall improvement must address the forest's score
decisions or the information supplied to it, rather than relying on admission
checks alone. That is an implication of the audit, not authorization or evidence
for another feature, threshold change, or deployment. No follow-on experiment
has been started.
